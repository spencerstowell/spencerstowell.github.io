Research area

Optimal spring design

Application

Powered lower-limb prostheses

Recognition

First-place student poster, 2026

Research direction

Designing for lower actuator load.

At BYU’s Compliant Mechanisms and Robotics Lab, I study compliant mechanisms and parallel elastic actuation for powered lower-limb prostheses. This work uses linear regression as a design tool for selecting springs that reduce actuator torque demands, with modeling and experimental validation.

The poster, “Linear Regression as an Intuitive Design Tool for Optimal Parallel Elastic Actuators,” received first place in the student poster competition at the 2026 BYU Biomedical Engineering Conference.

Related publication

Compliant bearing design and tests.

CORE bearings are a related compliant mechanism investigated for their potential as spring-and-bearing elements in parallel elastic actuator systems. The bearing study is a distinct contribution from the regression-based actuator design poster.
