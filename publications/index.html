Published work.

Rose, A., Stowell, S., Francom, E., Christiansen, A., Usevitch, N., & Howell, L. L. (2026). “Design and Experimental Validation of Compliant Rolling-Contact Element (CORE) Bearings.” Machines, 14(6), 600.

This work evaluates CORE bearings as compliant spring-and-bearing elements for parallel elastic actuator systems through design, finite element analysis, torque testing, optimization, and radial-stiffness experiments.
